About

I’m a first-year PhD student at the Universidad Nacional de Córdoba, Argentina, advised by Prof. Miguel Campercholi. From July 2026 to June 2027, I’m visiting the Computer Science Department of Cornell University as a research intern, where I’m working on proof systems under the supervision of Prof. Nick Spooner. I completed my Licenciatura in Computer Science (BSc+MSc equivalent) at UNC in 2025.

My research interests include computational complexity, coding theory, and cryptography in general.

Off the clock, I enjoy playing the piano, swimming, and practicing close-up card magic. The cards tend to win out, though — you’ll rarely see me without a deck nearby. I’m always happy to share a trick or two.
